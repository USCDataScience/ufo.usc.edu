Team 8 visualizations, summarizing the work our team did in exploring and investigating UFO sightings using data science

Group 8's DATAVIS submission for CSCI 599 Spring 2018 (authors: Matheos Asfaw, Teague Ashcraft, Eric Hachuel, Pablo Giudice, Bruno Mazetti). Interactive D3 visualizations and analysis of UFO sightings data including pop culture influence, severe weather events, OCR retention on British files, named entity recognition, word clouds from descriptions and UFOStalker metadata, sighting date circos, airbase radar, population factors, Tika vision object classification bubble charts, and more.
